History of Bangladesh

From Ancient Civilizations to Modern Nation

500 BCE

Ancient Civilizations

The region was part of ancient kingdoms like Gangaridai and was known for its muslin trade with Rome and China.

  • Mahasthangarh - oldest archaeological site
  • Buddhist and Hindu kingdoms flourished
  • Center of learning and trade
1576

Mughal Bengal

Bengal became the wealthiest province of the Mughal Empire, accounting for 12% of world GDP.

Achievements Impact
Textile Industry World's leading exporter
Shipbuilding Advanced maritime trade
1757

British Colonial Rule

The Battle of Plassey marked the beginning of British rule, leading to economic exploitation and famines.

"The Bengal famine was one of the greatest disasters of British rule."

1947

Partition & East Pakistan

Bengal was partitioned, with East Bengal becoming part of Pakistan despite cultural and linguistic differences.

1971

Liberation War & Independence

The Bangladesh Liberation War resulted in independence after 9 months of conflict.

Key Dates:
March 7: Historic Speech by Sheikh Mujibur Rahman
March 26: Independence Declaration
December 16: Victory Day
Present

Modern Bangladesh

Today, Bangladesh is a developing nation with significant economic growth and cultural influence.
